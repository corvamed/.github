<h3 align="center">A clear bridge to clinical trials.</h3>

Corvamed is an AI service in development. It will help patients and families find clinical trials they may qualify for and explain them in plain words. It will also help research teams reach interested, pre-checked participants. The service is not live yet.

### What the AI does

- Turns the rules of a trial (who can take part) into clear facts: age, diagnosis, stage, earlier treatment and reasons someone cannot take part.
- Compares those facts with the situation a person describes in their own words. When something is unclear, it says so instead of guessing.
- Explains each result in plain words and in the person's own language: what is tested, where, what it asks of you, the risks and questions for your doctor.

### What the AI does not do

- It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice.
- It does not decide who can take part. Only the trial doctor decides.
- It does not promise results. A trial is research, and you may receive a placebo.
- It does not share your details with a trial site until you say yes to that specific trial.
