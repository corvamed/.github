# Code of conduct

Corvamed works on a topic that matters a lot to patients and families. We want every space around the project to be respectful and safe.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not about the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.

## No real personal or medical data

Do not post real personal data or health information in issues, pull requests, discussions or files: not your own and not anyone else's. This includes medical records, diagnoses, test results and contact details of patients. Use made-up examples instead.

## Medical emergencies

This project cannot help in an emergency and does not give medical advice. If you or someone else needs urgent help, call your local emergency number (112 in the European Union) or contact a doctor.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks this code of conduct, email **info@corvamed.com**. We will read every report, treat it confidentially and respond.

We may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
