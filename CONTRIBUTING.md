# Contributing to Corvamed

Thank you for your interest in Corvamed. The project is in development, and we welcome helpful ideas and small fixes.

## Where to write

| Repository | What it is for | How to contribute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [responsible-ai](https://github.com/corvamed/responsible-ai) | How the AI in Corvamed is designed and the rules it follows | Open an issue with questions or suggestions |
| [corvamed-website](https://github.com/corvamed/corvamed-website) | Source of the website corvamed.com | Open an issue for errors, broken links or typos |
| [brand-assets](https://github.com/corvamed/brand-assets) | Official logos, colours and images | Email info@corvamed.com |
| [.github](https://github.com/corvamed/.github) | Organization profile and these community files | Email info@corvamed.com |

For anything else, including partnerships and investment, email **info@corvamed.com**.

## Issues

- Search existing issues first.
- One topic per issue, with a short, clear title.
- Describe what you expected and what happened.
- Never include real personal or health data. Use made-up examples.

## Pull requests

Small pull requests are welcome, for example fixes for typos, broken links or accessibility. For larger changes, please open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.

## Security and conduct

- To report a vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- Everyone taking part follows our [Code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
