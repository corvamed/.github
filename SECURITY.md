# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Corvamed and the people who use it safe.

## How to report a vulnerability

Email **privacy@corvamed.com** with the subject line **Security**.

Please include:

- what you found and where (repository, file or page);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what impact you think it has.

Please do **not** report vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests or discussions, and do not include real personal or health data in your report.

## What happens next

- We will confirm that we received your report.
- We will look into it, keep you updated and tell you when it is fixed.
- If you wish, we will credit you once the issue is resolved.

## Scope

Corvamed is in development and the service is not live yet. This policy covers the public repositories of the [Corvamed organization](https://github.com/corvamed) and the website [corvamed.com](https://corvamed.com).
